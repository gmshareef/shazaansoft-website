What's Inside

Everything your committee needs, in one app.

Eight connected modules covering the full life of your Jamaath — no more switching between tools.

Financial Management

Donations, monthly contributions, event collections and full ledgers — with automatic WhatsApp payment reminders.

Madarasa Management

Admissions, attendance, exams, timetables and awards for your Islamic school, in one system.

Asset & Rental Management

Track properties, rentals, leases and daily bookings, with security deposits and billing handled automatically.

Family & Member Records

A living family tree with documents, welfare history and benefit tracking, always kept up to date.

Events & Cultural Programs

Run competitions with tiers, teams and scoring, and generate results and reports instantly.

Staff & Payroll

Salaries, attendance and pay slips for your committee's staff, without spreadsheets.

WhatsApp & Receipt Printing

Send reminders and receipts straight from the app — digitally over WhatsApp, or on a thermal printer.

Built to Run Offline

A private, on-device system — no dependency on constant internet — with a full audit trail of every change.
